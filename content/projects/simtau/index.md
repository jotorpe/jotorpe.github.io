Software invention disclosure, LabTAU / INSERM (2026). Authors: J. Torres, V. Delattre, C. Lafon.

SimTAU couples acoustic and thermal models to plan and analyse HIFU treatments, and supports the
retrosimulation of delivered prostate cancer treatments compared against post-treatment MRI.
